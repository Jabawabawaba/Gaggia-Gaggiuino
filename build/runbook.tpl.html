Gaggia Install Runbook

The whole Gaggiuino install in eight phases, from the bench test to the first calibrated shot, written out step by step so you can follow it with only this page open. Each step says what you need, what to do in order and how to check it worked, with drawings and the official photos where they help, and a badge for what's powered while you do it. Tick a step when it's done and its instructions fold away. Set Your Gaggia in the top bar and your screen below, and you'll only see the steps for your machine. Before you open the machine, check you're ready with Test yourself.

Your screen

Not sure? The Gen 3.5 screen has black glass right to its edges; the Gen 3 screen's glass sits on an orange circuit board with a mounting hole in each corner (official photo). A kit sold as Gen 3.5 has the Gen 3.5 screen. Headless means no screen, just a small board you use over Wi-Fi.

Screen mount

Front sits on the front panel by magnets, funnel swivels on top of the water funnel, rear clips into the back vent (official photo). Until you pick, the screen steps show every version.
