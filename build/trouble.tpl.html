Gaggia Troubleshooting

Symptom first. Each one lists its likely causes in order and the checks that tell them apart. Check IDs like c3 are on the power-on checks, and wire names like J or Sa on the wiring page.

Unplug before you look inside

Every check here is done with the cord out of the wall and the machine, unless it says otherwise. A red "Unplug now" flag means stop using the machine until it's fixed. Never bridge the boiler's thermal fuse to get it running.

Still stuck

Include the code on your rating label, your voltage and install path, the firmware build numbers from Settings, About, and which power-on checks pass.
