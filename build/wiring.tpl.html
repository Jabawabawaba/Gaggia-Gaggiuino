Sources

  • The official Gaggiuino stock wiring integration and custom wiring guides and their schematics (PCB v3.1/v4), read September 2026. Connections, colors and lengths are as drawn on them. The seven stock-integration wire names are the guide's; the AC, G and LV run IDs are this site's.
  • Where a length isn't printed, the page says so. The official guide calls its lengths generous: route first, then trim.
  • The Shelly runs: fusio's "Gaggia Classic Pro + Shelly Plus 1PM Wiring" (220 to 240 V, PCB v3.1/v4.0), shared on the Gaggiuino Discord; unofficial. His drawing runs the machine's neutral through the Shelly's N terminals and feeds the power switch from the Shelly; Shelly's docs allow using its terminals as a bridge only at low currents, with the total under 15 A, and a 120 V Pro draws about 12 A on its own. So this site gives the Shelly its own neutral lead, keeps AC1 on the socket, and joins its output to AC2 and AC3 with a splice. The SH IDs are this site's.
