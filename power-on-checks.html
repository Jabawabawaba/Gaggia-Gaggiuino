Gaggia Power‑On Checks

Every check to pass before the converted Gaggia is plugged in for the first time, for your install path and schematic. Type in what the meter shows and each check marks itself pass or fail. Wire IDs like J, Sa or AC3 match the wiring page.

Unplugged for every step

Pull the cord from the wall and from the machine's inlet before you start, and keep it out until every check passes. A basic multimeter catches shorts, open circuits and wrong connections, but it can't test insulation under voltage. For first power-on, use a GFCI-protected outlet and keep the covers on.

With the Shelly, its own checks are added and the ones it replaces drop out. Once it's plugged in, the socket, the Shelly, the power switch and their wires are live even with the machine off. The relay breaks line only, so the rest of the machine stays joined to the outlet's neutral, which a plug that fits either way round can make line, and the app or a schedule can switch it on at any time.
