Gaggia Wire Labels

A flag label for each end of every new wire, using the IDs from the wiring page. Each label says which end it sits on and where the other end goes, so you can check a wire without tracing it. On Plug-and-Play wiring each end gets its own official label (L1a on one end, L1b on the other), as on the official cut list. For stock integration there are flags for the stock connectors the guide names, too.

Print and fit

  1. Print the PDF at 100% (actual size), not "fit to page". The bar at the top right of each sheet should measure 50 mm.
  2. Plain paper with clear tape over it works. Full-sheet sticker paper is quicker.
  3. Cut on the solid lines. Label each wire as you cut and crimp it, before it goes into the machine.
  4. Wrap the blank middle strip around the wire about 3 cm from the end, fold the two halves together, text outward. Seal with tape if it's plain paper.
  5. Each wire's row has two labels: the left one goes on the first-named end, the right one on the other. Stock connector flags go on the stock wire, just behind its connector. A kit harness may come labeled already: check its labels against these.

Mark by hand

    These ends sit on or beside the boiler, where paper and tape adhesive won't last. Write the ID on the silicone jacket with an oil-based permanent marker, which the official guide recommends for silicone.
